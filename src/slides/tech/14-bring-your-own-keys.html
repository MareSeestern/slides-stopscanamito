Source. Amito toolkit, Oct 2026. Providers and settings as in its configuration template.

  • Amito doesn't lock anyone into one AI vendor. Whoever runs it, a newsroom, a fact-checking team or a school, connects the model they prefer with their own API key. OpenAI, Claude, or a local model.
  • The key stays with them and so does the bill. With a local model there is no key at all, and the images never leave the organisation's computer. We tested Mistral Small 3.2 with Ollama on one GPU.
  • Switching is one setting in the configuration, the provider, its key and the model.
  • The model only runs the conversation and chooses checks. The verdict comes from the trust report and its written profile, so changing the model doesn't change the rules. Today Claude runs Amito as a free agent, and the other models follow the scripted STOP&SCAN route.
