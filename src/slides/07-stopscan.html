Rules that shape the inputs

IPTC Photo Metadata Standard. The placement under Source, Content and Alignment is the STOP&SCAN mapping.Demirtas et al., SPIE 2026 manuscript, 18 Aug 2026. Figure 1 is the current framework. Factuality is proposed, not in the standard.

  • Five elements. STOP and Now Reflect bookend the process and hold no tools. Without a recorded starting point, a person can't tell reasoning from rationalising.
  • Provenance is a strong input to Source. One channel among several, not the answer.
  • The output is an evidence state, Confirmed, Contradicted or Unresolved, plus an action. Not a real-or-fake verdict.
  • Beat 2. Amito could run the method-bearing steps. It is a prototype, and the tech team picks this up shortly.
  • Beat 3, IPTC. Every IPTC field is a declaration, and STOP&SCAN says which step tests it. Source reads who declares they made it and how, from Creator and Credit Line to Digital Source Type and AI System Used. Content has no field. The pixels are tested against what Digital Source Type declares. Alignment tests when and where, Date Created, the two location fields and Exif GPS, against independent records. The Malmö demo runs that row live, GPS and landmark against the weather.
  • Beat 3, JPEG Trust. Figure 1 of Demirtas et al. shows the current indicators. Source takes provenance, attribution and rights, and metadata. Content takes the media content. Watermarks are also on that figure and are not given a step here. Alignment takes factuality, which they propose and which is not in the framework yet. Factuality means the story, and whether this image is from that event, time or place. It is an author manuscript for SPIE 2026, not a published standard.
