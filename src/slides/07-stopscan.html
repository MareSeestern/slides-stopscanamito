Rules that shape the inputs

  • Five elements. STOP and Now Reflect bookend the process and hold no tools. Without a recorded starting point, a person can't tell reasoning from rationalising.
  • Provenance is a strong input to Source. One channel among several, not the answer.
  • The output is an evidence state, Confirmed, Contradicted or Unresolved, plus an action. Not a real-or-fake verdict.
  • Beat 2. Amito could run the method-bearing steps. It is a prototype, and the tech team picks this up shortly.
  • Beat 3, for this room. Every IPTC field is a declaration, and STOP&SCAN says which step tests it. Source reads who declares they made it and how, from Creator and Credit Line to Digital Source Type and AI System Used. Content has no field. The pixels are tested against what Digital Source Type declares. Alignment tests when and where, Date Created, the two location fields and Exif GPS, against independent records. The Malmö demo runs that row live, GPS and landmark against the weather.
